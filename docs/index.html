Case → policy evidence packages

Three privacy-safe packages are generated in the final CI benchmark.

Each package connects a pseudonymous investigation candidate to its reason codes, the relevant registered rule family, active data-quality caveats, replay/evidence-power/resilience context, current experiment state, competing benign explanations, and escalation/privacy gates. A package is a decision aid — never an abuse verdict.

Why the project does not stop at a classifier

Known abuse

Frozen rules, risk/anomaly signals, legitimate-user controls, FPR/FNR and evidence-volume gates.
Unknown abuse

Recent-vs-baseline novelty, behavior cohorts, telemetry-health screening and candidate taxonomy proposals.
Adaptive behavior

Rule brittleness and defense-in-depth stress without exposing real production bypass thresholds.
Policy impact

Pre-period-only cluster randomization, ITT, placebo/negative control, displacement, sequential guardrails and human stopping decisions.

Safety and governance boundary

No automatic enforcement. No automatic policy expansion.

The default analyst layer excludes raw prompts, completions, raw IP addresses, raw device identifiers, card numbers and payment details. Scores, rules, cohorts, experiments, registries and evidence packages support human investigation and policy review only.
