# Queue operations

Risk scores become useful only when connected to analyst capacity.

The benchmark maps flagged cases to P0–P3 tiers, synthetic SLA targets, queue age, estimated review minutes, and risk-per-review-minute priority. Capacity scenarios report how many cases can be reviewed under 4, 8, 16, or 32 analyst hours and the expected benchmark precision/known-abuse recall.

These review-time estimates are synthetic planning assumptions, not staffing claims. In production they should be replaced by measured case-handling distributions split by case type, complexity, and escalation path.
