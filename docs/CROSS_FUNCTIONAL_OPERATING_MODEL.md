# Cross-functional operating model

## Trust & Safety
Owns investigation policy, case outcomes, enforcement review, and feedback about false-positive harm.

## Analytics / Data Science
Owns measurement design, monitoring, threshold/rule evaluation, calibration, review-capacity analysis, and uncertainty communication.

## Engineering / Security
Owns reliable telemetry, identity/auth lifecycle signals, detection implementation, monitoring reliability, and safe rollback paths.

## Product
Owns entitlement/product context and mitigation rollout decisions.

## Privacy / Legal review
Reviews new purposes, sensitive-data access, expanded identity linkage, raw-content access, retention/sharing changes, and any proposed move toward automatic enforcement.

The `stakeholder_action_register.csv` connects each finding to an owner, partners, next action, and review gate.
